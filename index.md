## Bio
I am an incoming CS PhD student at Johns Hopkins University, where I will be co-advised by [Dr. Steven Salzberg](https://salzberg-lab.org/) and [Dr. Mihaela Pertea](https://ccb.jhu.edu/people/mpertea/). My research interests span computational genomics, AI for science, and long-context LLMs.

I hold an MS in Computational Biology from Carnegie Mellon University, where I worked with [Dr. Carl Kingsford](https://kingsfordlab.cbd.cmu.edu/index.html), and a BS from National Taiwan University, advised by [Dr. Chien-Yu Chen](https://c4lab.github.io/).

Previously, I worked at Genentech as a Computational Biology Intern, Taiwan AI Labs as a Machine Learning Engineer Intern, and Missmi Intelligence as a Part-time Software Engineer.

## Industry Experience

- Software Engineer (Part-time) at Missmi Intelligence: Built gene reporting products from WGS, WES, and targeted sequencing secondary analysis, collaborating with doctors on clinical action plans.
